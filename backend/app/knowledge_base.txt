General Information:
A visa is an official document that allows the bearer to legally enter a foreign country. The visa is usually stamped or glued into the bearer's passport.

Tourist Visa:
A tourist visa is for personal travel, leisure, or visiting friends and family. It does not permit the holder to engage in any business activities. Applicants usually need to show proof of funds, a return ticket, and strong ties to their home country, like a job or property.

Student Visa:
A student visa is for individuals who want to study abroad at an accredited educational institution. Applicants must provide an acceptance letter from the school, proof of financial support for tuition and living expenses, and proof of ties to their home country.

Work Visa:
A work visa is required for individuals who intend to take up employment in a foreign country. This type of visa is often sponsored by an employer in the destination country. Requirements include a job offer, a signed contract, and proof that the applicant has the necessary skills and qualifications for the job.
